Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At Properties-M ("we", "us", "our"), protecting the privacy of our high-net-worth clients, prospective buyers, and site visitors is of utmost importance. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, utilize, and protect your information when using our website and advisory services.

1. Information We Collect

When you request a private portfolio, consultation, or contact us via WhatsApp or telephone, we may collect:

  • Your full name and title.
  • Contact phone number (including WhatsApp numbers).
  • Email address.
  • Property preferences (budget range, preferred locations like New Cairo, Zayed, Sahel, Sokhna, Mostakbal, New Capital, and developer preferences).

2. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your data exclusively to:

  • Provide personalized real estate advisory and prepare customized property dossiers.

3. Zero Spam & Strict Confidentiality

We operate under strict confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third-party marketing companies. Information is only shared with authorized developer representatives when you explicitly request a formal reservation or contract issuance.

We may use cookies and analytics tools (including Google Analytics and Google Ads) to understand traffic patterns and assess marketing conversions. You may adjust your browser settings to decline cookies at any time.
